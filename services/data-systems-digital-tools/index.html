What we do

  1. 1

    List what you hold

    We list the records you hold, where they sit and who uses them, then agree what one system must do.

  2. 2

    Choose the tool

    We choose with you between Excel, Access, SharePoint and Power Apps, SQL or a custom web app, based on how many records you hold, who uses them and the tools you already have.

  3. 3

    Build the database and forms

    We build the database and web forms, with checks as data is entered and access levels for each user, and link it to KoBoToolbox for field data.

  4. 4

    Automate the reports

    We set up the reports, alerts and dashboards you need, produced from the database without retyping.

  5. 5

    Build your website, if you need one

    We build a website adapted to your business that shows your clients' problems and how you solve them, instead of a list of services, with forms linked to your database.

  6. 6

    Protect and hand over

    We set the data protection settings, write a user guide, train your staff and hand over full control.

Who it's for

Beneficiary or household records, distributions and services, partners and their reports, cases and referrals, or staff, assets and stock. Built for office staff, field teams, partners and managers who need dashboards.

Clients and contacts, suppliers and subcontractors, projects and contracts, bids and tenders, or stock and equipment. Built for office and site staff, managers, and subcontractors or clients who need reports.

How an engagement runs

  1. 1

    Take the problem check: answer a short multiple-choice check online. We reply within 24 hours and assign one named consultant.

  2. 2

    Scoping call: a free 45-minute consultation. We listen first: your context, constraints, and donor or contract requirements.

  3. 3

    Proposal: scope, deliverables, team, timeline and price in writing, within 3 days.

  4. 4

    Diagnosis: we list the records you hold, where they sit and who uses them, then agree what one system must do.

  5. 5

    Build and test: we build the system with your staff and test it with real records before you switch over.

  6. 6

    Handover and follow-up: training, guides and full handover so your staff can run it themselves, then a follow-up check 30 days later.
