Project management, data, operations and tender support for NGOs, UN agencies and companies across Jordan, the Gulf and the region, by people who have done this work.

EBAL is a management consulting company registered in Jordan, serving NGOs, UN agencies and private-sector companies across Jordan, the Gulf and the wider region. We work in nine domains: project management and development; data collection and analysis; information management and reporting; monitoring, evaluation and accountability; operations and administration; business development; tenders and document control; databases and data systems; and engineering support, covering bills of quantities (BOQs), site supervision and works monitoring. Our team has run projects, data systems and operations itself, so each engagement ends with deliverables your staff can use and keep up to date.

Find the service that fits your problem

A late donor report and a rejected bid often have causes that can be fixed, such as data spread across too many tools or procedures built for a smaller organisation. Each service below starts from a problem that NGOs, UN agencies or companies face. Find the one closest to yours and book a consultation to scope the engagement.

Tenders, engineering and document control

Matches your answer

The need

Preparing a tender or a bid takes weeks of specifications, BOQs and compliance documents, and one missing form can disqualify a bid or force a re-tender. Once works start, the client needs someone on site checking progress and quality, and a document trail that holds up at handover, payment and audit.

What we do

We organise tender processes for NGOs, UN agencies and private-sector companies that issue tenders, and we prepare bids, including BOQs, for contractors and suppliers bidding on them. During works, we supervise sites with our licensed engineering partner where required, monitor progress and quality against the BOQ and contract specifications, and run document control from tender to handover.

What you get

  • Tender package (invitation, specifications, BOQ, evaluation criteria) and evaluation report
  • Bid package with priced BOQ and compliance documents checked against the tender requirements
  • Site supervision and works monitoring reports on progress and quality
  • Document control register and filing system covering tender, contract, variations and handover

Data systems and digital tools

Matches your answer

The need

Client, supplier and subcontractor details sit in scattered spreadsheets and email threads, so nobody has one up-to-date list and the same data is typed again for every report or bid.   • Web forms with user access levels
  • Automated reports, alerts and dashboards
  • Data protection settings, user guide and staff training
  • Website built around your clients' problems and your solutions, with forms linked to your data

How we work

1Take the problem check 2Scoping call 3Proposal 4Diagnosis 5Build and test 6Handover and follow-up
  1. 1

    Take the problem check

    Answer a short multiple-choice problem check online; skip any question you like. We reply within 24 hours and assign one named consultant.

    You receive: Your problem profile, and one named consultant as your single contact

  2. 2

    Scoping call

    Free consultation. We listen first: your context, constraints, and donor or contract requirements.

    You receive: A short note of the problem as we understand it

  3. 3

    Proposal

    Scope, deliverables, team, timeline and price, shaped to what you need.

    You receive: A written proposal within 3 days

  4. 4

    Diagnosis

    Root cause analysis with your team before we design anything.

    You receive: Findings note: what is causing the problem

  5. 5

    Build and test

    We build the solution with your staff and test it in real conditions.

    You receive: The agreed deliverables: tools, SOPs, data, reports or systems

  6. 6

    Handover and follow-up

    Training, guides and full handover so your staff can run it themselves.

    You receive: Handover pack + follow-up check 30 days after handover
