Project development and management

Funding calls leave little time to make the concept note, logframe and budget agree. After signing, workplans, spending and staffing drift apart. We help you write the proposal, then set up the plans and tracking that keep them in step.

What we do

  1. 1

    Agree the project logic

    Before anyone writes the full proposal, we agree the project logic, logframe and budget storyline with your team.

  2. 2

    Write the proposal

    We write the concept note or full proposal, with the logframe and budget narrative, in the format your donor asks for.

  3. 3

    Find where plans drift

    For a project already running, we put the workplan, spending and staffing side by side, find where they drift apart and assess the risks.

  4. 4

    Set up the plan and tracker

    We build a detailed workplan with milestones and a tracker, in Microsoft Project or your own tools, that links activities, budget and staffing.

  5. 5

    Prepare start-up and close-out

    We give you checklists for the first months and for close-out, and train your staff to keep the tracker up to date.

Who it's for

NGOs and UN agencies

Programme and grants teams preparing a concept note or full proposal for a funding call, and project managers whose workplan, spending and staffing no longer match. All four deliverables apply.

Private-sector companies

Companies running several projects that need a detailed workplan, a tracker linking costs, schedule and staffing, and start-up and close-out checklists. Preparing a bid for a tender? See Tenders, engineering and document control.

How an engagement runs

  1. 1

    Take the problem check: answer a short multiple-choice check online. We reply within 24 hours and assign one named consultant.

  2. 2

    Scoping call: a free 45-minute consultation. We listen first: your context, constraints, and donor or contract requirements.

  3. 3

    Proposal: scope, deliverables, team, timeline and price in writing, within 3 days.

  4. 4

    Diagnosis: we test the project logic before the proposal is written, or put your workplan, spending and staffing side by side to find where they drift apart.

  5. 5

    Build and test: we write the proposal or build the workplan and tracker with your staff, then test the tracker on your live project.

  6. 6

    Handover and follow-up: training, guides and full handover so your staff can run it themselves, then a follow-up check 30 days later.
