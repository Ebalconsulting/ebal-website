Data collection, analysis and reporting

Matches your answer

The need

You need baseline or assessment data from the field within weeks, but you have no trained enumerators or tested digital forms, and no way to check quality while teams are still out. When the data comes in, it sits next to Excel sheets and partner reports that count beneficiaries differently, so staff spend days reconciling figures before each donor report.

What we do

We plan and run field data collection, from sampling and digital forms to enumerator training and daily supervision. We then clean and analyse the data, report it in the format your donor or management expects, and set up how information is stored and shared.

What you get

  • Sampling plan, questionnaires and tested digital forms in KoBoToolbox
  • Field data collection by a trained, supervised enumerator team with daily quality checks
  • Cleaned dataset with a data dictionary and information management plan
  • Analysis report, Power BI dashboard and reporting templates

Tenders, engineering and document control

Matches your answer

The need

Preparing a tender or a bid takes weeks of specifications, BOQs and compliance documents, and one missing form can disqualify a bid or force a re-tender. Once works start, the client needs someone on site checking progress and quality, and a document trail that holds up at handover, payment and audit.

What we do

We organise tender processes for NGOs, UN agencies and private-sector companies that issue tenders, and we prepare bids, including BOQs, for contractors and suppliers bidding on them. During works, we supervise sites with our licensed engineering partner where required, monitor progress and quality against the BOQ and contract specifications, and run document control from tender to handover.

What you get

  • Tender package (invitation, specifications, BOQ, evaluation criteria) and evaluation report
  • Bid package with priced BOQ and compliance documents checked against the tender requirements
  • Site supervision and works monitoring reports on progress and quality
  • Document control register and filing system covering tender, contract, variations and handover

Data systems and digital tools

Matches your answer

The need

Client, supplier and subcontractor details sit in scattered spreadsheets and email threads, so nobody has one up-to-date list and the same data is typed again for every report or bid. NGOs keep beneficiary, partner and distribution records in separate files that cannot be checked for duplicates or shared safely.

What we do

We design and build databases and web-based systems that keep your data in one place, check it as it is entered and produce the reports you need automatically. We connect them to the tools you already use, train your staff and hand over full control. We also build websites adapted to your business that show your clients' problems and how you solve them, rather than a list of services.

What you get

  • Database for clients, suppliers and subcontractors, or for beneficiaries, partners and distributions, built in Excel, Access, SharePoint and Power Apps, SQL or a custom web app
  • Links to KoBoToolbox for field data
  • Web forms with user access levels
  • Automated reports, alerts and dashboards
  • Data protection settings, user guide and staff training
  • Website built around your clients' problems and your solutions, with forms linked to your data
