How to contract EBAL

  1. 1

    Send us your RFQ, RFP or tender, or take the problem check

  2. 2

    Free 45-minute consultation to confirm scope

  3. 3

    Written technical and financial proposal within 3 days, in English or Arabic

  4. 4

    We complete your vendor registration and compliance forms

  5. 5

    Contract or purchase order

  6. 6

    Delivery, handover and a follow-up check 30 days later

Engineering partner

Where licensing requires it, site supervision is delivered with our licensed engineering partner. Our proposal sets out the partner's role, and we ask you before we share any of your information with them.
