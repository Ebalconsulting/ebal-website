What we do

  1. 1

    Agree what you need

    We agree what data you need, who will use it and how it will be checked.

  2. 2

    Plan the sample and forms

    We write the sampling plan and questionnaires and build digital forms in KoBoToolbox, tested before field work starts.

  3. 3

    Collect and check

    Our trained enumerator team collects the data. Our data collection consultants supervise the team and run quality checks every day while it is still in the field.

  4. 4

    Clean and analyse

    We clean the dataset, describe every variable in a data dictionary and analyse the results.

  5. 5

    Report and organise

    We report in the format your donor or management expects, build a Power BI dashboard and reporting templates, and set out how information is stored and shared.

Already have the data? We start at step 4: we trace where each figure comes from and why your sources differ, then agree one way to count.

Who it's for

NGOs and UN agencies

Baselines, needs assessments, household surveys, interviews and focus group discussions, site or market observation, and post-distribution monitoring. Also for teams that must reconcile figures from forms, Excel and partner reports before a donor report.

Private-sector companies

Market and customer surveys, surveys or assessments a client or funder requires, site or facility assessments, and dashboards that managers can use.

How an engagement runs

  1. 1

    Take the problem check: answer a short multiple-choice check online. We reply within 24 hours and assign one named consultant.

  2. 2

    Scoping call: a free 45-minute consultation. We listen first: your context, constraints, and donor or contract requirements.

  3. 3

    Proposal: scope, deliverables, team, timeline and price in writing, within 3 days.

  4. 4

    Diagnosis: we agree what data you need and how it will be checked, or trace why your existing figures differ.

  5. 5

    Build and test: we test the forms, train the enumerators, collect and check the data, then clean, analyse and report it.

  6. 6

    Handover and follow-up: training, guides and full handover so your staff can run it themselves, then a follow-up check 30 days later.
