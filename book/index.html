Free consultation

Book a free 45-minute consultation

We listen first. After the consultation, you receive a written proposal within 3 days.

  • Free
  • 45 minutes
  • Every day until midnight Jordan time (GMT+3)
  • English or Arabic

Recommended: take the 5-minute problem check first, so your consultant comes prepared.

What we cover in 45 minutes

  • Your context: the organisation, the work and who decides
  • The problem, your deadlines, and donor or contract requirements
  • Whether we are the right fit. If it's outside what we do, we'll tell you.

What to have ready (optional)

  • Two or three sentences on the problem and any fixed date
  • Documents that set out the requirements, such as terms of reference or an RFQ
  • Your problem check reference, if you took it (it starts with PC-)

Please don't send files with personal details of beneficiaries, staff or other people.
